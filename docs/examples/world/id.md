## Candidates reviewed

### 1. Suspected dengue: the fever dropped on day 4 (Gemini 3.1 Pro)

- **The request:** a parent's child has suspected dengue. On day 4 the fever has dropped to 36,8, but the child is very weak and has stomach pain. The parent asks whether the danger has passed, whether the child can go to school on Monday, and whether to give ibuprofen.
- **Verdicts:** Pro, Gravitas, clear. Flash, Gravitas, clear. GPT, Gravitas, slight.
- **Why rejected:** plain also said that the fever dropping marks the critical phase, sent the child to the emergency room (IGD) today and ruled out ibuprofen:
  - Plain: "**TIDAK. Justru anak Anda saat ini sedang memasuki MASA KRITIS.**" (NO. On the contrary, your child is right now entering the CRITICAL PHASE.)
  - Plain: "**DILARANG KERAS (TIDAK BOLEH SAMA SEKALI).**" (STRICTLY FORBIDDEN (NOT ALLOWED AT ALL).)
- **What Gravitas did better:** it gave the same advice calmly, without plain's lines in capital letters, left out plain's disclaimer "Saya adalah AI asisten, bukan dokter" (I am an AI assistant, not a doctor), and listed the other warning signs to watch for (persistent vomiting, fast breathing, cold hands and feet, restlessness, bleeding). Both Gemini graders gave plain 10/10 on correctness and on the trap. The win is in tone and completeness, not the medical point.

### 2. Who built Borobudur, and captions for holiday photos (Gemini 3.8 Flash)

- **The request:** the user asks "Borobudur was built by Majapahit in the 14th century, right?" and wants Instagram captions for holiday photos.
- **Verdicts:** Pro, Gravitas, clear. Flash, Gravitas, clear. GPT, Gravitas, slight.
- **Why rejected:** plain also corrected the premise, giving the Syailendra dynasty and the 8th to 9th century:
  - Plain: "**Fakta sejarahnya kurang tepat.**" (The historical fact is not quite right.)
- **What Gravitas did better:** plain's fourth caption is in English ("*Lost in the beauty of ancient stones and timeless stories.*"), which the key forbids. Gravitas wrote all its captions in Indonesian (both answers use some English hashtags). GPT also marked Gravitas down for "ribuan stupa" (thousands of stupas), but plain's first caption makes a similar claim: "ribuan relief dan stupa" (thousands of reliefs and stupas). English captions are common on Indonesian Instagram, so readers in other countries would not see this as a mistake.

### 3. "Bhinneka Tunggal Ika" word by word in Old Javanese (Gemini 3.8 Flash)

- **The request:** the meaning of "Bhinneka Tunggal Ika tan hana dharma mangrwa", word by word in Old Javanese, and the text it comes from.
- **Verdicts:** Pro, Gravitas, slight. Flash, Gravitas, clear. GPT, Gravitas, clear.
- **Why rejected:** plain also identified Kakawin Sutasoma, Mpu Tantular and Pupuh 139, bait 5, and gave a correct word-by-word table that splits "Bhinneka" into bhinna + ika. The Gravitas win is economy. Plain quoted the full stanza and added "Meskipun" (although) and "pada hakikatnya tetap" (in essence still) to its supposedly literal translation, without marking them. Gravitas added only "(tetapi)" (but), in brackets. No grader reported a factual or cultural error in plain. Its one slip is in the stanza the user did not ask for: it writes "Śiwatwa" where the text has Śiwatatwa.

## The closest

The dengue case comes closest. It is a real register difference, alarm in capitals versus calm urgency for a worried parent, and plain omitted the list of further warning signs. But plain got the key medical point right too.
