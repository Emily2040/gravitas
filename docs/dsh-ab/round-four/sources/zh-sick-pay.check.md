## 2. "800元低于任何一个合法口径". **Refuted / needs change (too absolute).**
This holds only if 800 is the gross amount (应发). Fact 12 of the same key concedes the other case: if 2192 is read as gross, take-home pay after personal social insurance (10.5%) and housing fund can be about 790 to 950. Worked example: 2192 − 840 − 8000×7% = 792. So 800 take-home is not below "every" lawful reading. It is below the reading most sources take (net, per the old 14号 and the CMS note). HR's claim "可以不发" is wrong whichever reading applies.
- https://cms.law/en/chn/legal-updates/key-changes-to-shanghai-sick-leave-pay-standards-what-employers-should-know (2026-09-29, secondary). Its summary says the 2192 floor is not reduced by personal contributions and calls this the prevailing view.
- https://rsj.sh.gov.cn/tmsztc_17502/20240416/t0035_1423583.html
  > 目前的缴费比例分别为：基本养老保险8%、基本医疗保险2%、失业保险0.5%，共计10.5%。
- **Corrected wording:** 如果800元是应发数，它低于任何合法口径，属于少发工资。如果800元是扣除个人社保和公积金后的实发数：按14号文原口径和多数意见（最低标准不含个人缴费），实发也不得低于2192元，800元同样违法；只有按"2192元为税前应发"的少数口径，实发才可能在八百元上下，而这一口径没有官方认可。无论哪种口径，"病假可以不发工资"都是错的。先确认工资条上的800元是应发还是实发。

## 9. Daily formula, base ÷ 21.75 × rate × sick working days; "整月病假即为月数额"; 2192 ÷ 21.75 ≈ 100.78. **Needs change (minor).**
The formula, the 21.75 divisor and 100.78 are confirmed (2192 / 21.75 = 100.78; 8000 / 21.75 × 0.8 = 294.25). The sentence "整月病假即为月数额" has no source. The bureau formula multiplies by the actual number of sick working days. September 2026 has 22 working days: 21 weekdays that are not holidays, plus Sunday 9-20, which is a make-up working day. 9-25 is a statutory holiday.
- https://rsj.sh.gov.cn/trdhy_17355/20221010/t0035_1410501.html. The page itself is dated 2022-09-05, not 2022-10-10 as the source file says.
  > 病假工资=计算基数÷月计薪天数×计算系数×病假天数
- 沪人社规〔2026〕10号 Art. 14: 「日工资按月工资除以每月平均计薪天数 21.75 天计算」.
- State Council 2026 holiday notice: https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/202511/content_7047090.htm
  > 中秋节：9月25日（周五）至27日（周日）放假，共3天。
  > 国庆节：10月1日（周四）至7日（周三）放假调休，共7天。9月20日（周日）、10月10日（周六）上班。
- Per-day arithmetic for September 2026 (22 sick working days): 22 × 294.25 = 6473.56 under the company tier; 22 × 100.78 = 2217.20 at the floor.
- **Corrected wording:** 按日计算：病假工资 = 基数 ÷ 21.75 × 系数 × 病假工作日数（剔除休息日、节假日）。2026年9月有22个工作日（含9月20日周日调休上班），9月25日中秋为法定节假日。整月病假大致等于月数额，严格按日公式会略有出入（按22天计：底线约2217元，公司若采用80%分档约6474元）；法定节假日当天怎么发，未找到上海官方口径。2192元是整月的最低保障，整月病假实得不应低于它。不满整月的按工作日折算，按底线每个病假工作日约100.78元。

## 12. Social insurance continues during sick leave; personal share 10.5%; about 840 on an 8000 base; "实发可能只剩八九百元". **Needs change (minor).**
- 840 is correct (8000 × 10.5%). 医疗 2% for 2026 is now confirmed from the primary page, not just a search snippet: 沪医保规〔2026〕2号, https://ybj.sh.gov.cn/gfxwj/20260313/921e047144694b61b6df8ca0c5ef2cfc.html, 「个人缴费比例为2%。」
- 8% and 0.5% are confirmed only by the 2024-03-18 bureau Q&A quoted under fact 2.
- The 2026 base range is 7546 to 37731 from 2026-07-01 (https://rsj.sh.gov.cn/tgsgg_17341/20260818/t0035_1443203.html: 「下限调整为7546元/月」), so an 8000 base is possible.
- The range is off. With the unverified 5% to 7% housing-fund rate, 2192 − 840 − (400 to 560) = **792 to 952**, which is "七百九十多到九百五十元左右", not 八九百. The 800 on the payslip sits inside this range. That matters for fact 2.
- Nothing I opened directly supports the clause "按缴费基数代扣，不按病假工资计算".
- **Corrected wording:** ……缴费基数若为8000元，个人社保约840元；公积金按公司比例另计（上海常见5%–7%，未核实，约400–560元）。如果公司按2192元应发再扣个人社保和公积金，实发约790–950元，与工资条上的800元相当；这种做法是否合规，取决于第6条尚无官方口径的争议。
