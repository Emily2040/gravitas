Before the email — one thing worth checking, because it's $3,400 of your money: **Jiffy Lube oil changes almost certainly did not void your powertrain warranty.** Under the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act (15 U.S.C. § 2302(c)), a manufacturer can't condition coverage on you using its branded parts or dealership service unless it provides them free, and it can't deny a claim just because someone else did routine maintenance. To deny you, Honda has to show the outside maintenance *caused* the transmission failure. Engine oil changes have no path to a slipping transmission.

So the dealer's explanation is probably wrong, and saying "that's on me" would hand them a concession they haven't earned. Here's an email that asks for the price break without giving that up. If you'd rather just pay and be done, tell me and I'll strip out the warranty part.
