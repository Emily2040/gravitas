Two quick notes before you send:

**The agent's explanation wasn't accurate.** Under the U.S. DOT's refund rules, if an airline cancels a flight — for any reason, including weather — and the passenger chooses not to travel, the passenger is entitled to a refund to the original form of payment, not just a credit. Weather affects what the airline owes you in *compensation* and rebooking, not whether you can get your money back. If you'd rather have the $412, you can still ask for it, and the DOT complaint process is available if they push back.

**On the one-year ask:** that's a reasonable and standard request — United's travel credits are typically valid for one year from issuance. Asking for written confirmation is the useful part, since it pins down the expiration date and the amount.
