**Two corrections first.** The renewal fee is **$130**, not $110 — it rose to $130 on December 27, 2021 and is still $130 as of October 2026. And two weeks is optimistic: the State Department's posted times are **4–6 weeks for routine service** and **2–3 weeks for expedited** (+$60), and both exclude mailing time at each end ([processing times](https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/apply/help/processing-time.html), [2026 fee reporting](https://www.northjersey.com/story/news/2026/10/15/passports-with-trumps-image-may-be-coming-soon-how-you-can-get-one/92260819007/)).

## Checklist

1. **Confirm you can renew by mail.** Your last passport must have been issued when you were 16+, issued within the last 15 years, undamaged, and in your current name (or you have the marriage certificate/court order). If any fail, you need Form DS-11 in person and $165.
2. **Form DS-82.** Fill it out online, print it, sign and date it. One form per person.
3. **Your most recent passport book** (and card, if you're renewing that too).
4. **One new 2×2 photo**, taken within the last 6 months, plain white background, no glasses.
5. **Name-change document**, if applicable — original or certified copy.
6. **Payment:** check or money order payable to **"U.S. Department of State"** for **$130**. Add $60 for expedited service — $130 + $60 = $190. Write your full name and date of birth on it. No cash, no credit cards by mail.
7. **Mail it trackably** — a large envelope, USPS Priority Mail or similar, to the address on the DS-82 instructions for your state. Write **EXPEDITE** on the envelope if you paid for it.
8. **After mailing:** track at [passportstatus.state.gov](https://passportstatus.state.gov/). Your old passport comes back separately, often a few weeks after the new one.

**Budget:** $130 routine. Expedited is $130 + $60 = $190. Optional 1–3 day return delivery adds $26.87.

**One thing that changes your plan:** if you travel within 14 days, don't mail it at all — call 1-877-487-2778 for an urgent-travel appointment at a regional agency.
