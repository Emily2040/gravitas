# Adversarial check: zh-passport-remote-apply.md, item checks and "Facts for the key"

Checked 2026-10-25 into 2026-10-26 by an agent that did not write the source file. On disk I read only `prompts/zh-passport-remote-apply.txt` and `sources/zh-passport-remote-apply.md`. I downloaded every URL below myself with curl and converted it to text. I did not reuse any download the source author made. Quotes are verbatim, in the original language, two lines at most. Some NIA pages put stray spaces around numbers and quotation marks, and I drop those spaces here. Each fact gets a status: **stands** or **refuted/needs change**. F-numbers follow the source's item numbers.

Newer-rule search: I found no rule, amendment, figure or announcement up to 2026-10-26 that makes the premise true. 全国通办 for ordinary passports still applies. Shenzhen's live service guide gives its 通办范围 as "全国" and accepts non-Guangdong residents. The State Council's new 《国务院关于出境入境管理的规定》 (in force 2026-09-15) does not touch where to apply, issue times or fees. I read all 19 articles on Xinhua (F19). I did find one newer measure the source missed. NIA announcement 2025年第4号 (2025-11-03) expanded online renewal and replacement (全程网办) from 20 to 50 pilot cities from 2025-11-20 (F13, F20). It covers 换发补发 only, and 周口 is not on the list, so the user's case does not change. Other checks:
- The NIA 通知公告 listing, newest item 2026-10-18, paged back to January 2025, shows no other passport measure.
- The 移民管理要闻 listing, newest item 2026-10-20, shows nothing new. The 2026-10-20 press conference only gave Q3 data.
- The NIA 政策解读 listing, newest item 2026-09-21, shows nothing new.
- No 2027 holiday notice is on gov.cn "最新政策" (newest item 2026-10-23).
- No change to the Singapore 30-day visa exemption was found. The ICA visa page was updated 25 June 2026, and China is still not on it.
- Searches for an amendment to the 护照法 or a new passport fee found nothing.

Tooling note: the shared WebSearch budget ran out partway through. After that I only fetched known primary URLs directly. Two searches never ran: an exact-phrase search for "2027年部分节假日安排", and one for any 2026 measure allowing first passports online. I covered both by reading the gov.cn and NIA listings directly.

## The premise

**F1. Since 2019-04-01, ordinary passports are 全国通办. A resident can apply at any exit-entry window, with the same procedure as at the 户籍地.**
Status: **stands**
- URL: https://www.nia.gov.cn/n741440/n741577/c1015032/content.html (国家移民管理局, 政策解读, 2019-03-26)
- Quote: "自2019年4月1日起，中华人民共和国普通护照、往来港澳通行证、往来台湾通行证等出入境证件实行“全国通办”，即内地居民可在全国任一出入境管理窗口申请办理上述出入境证件，申办手续与户籍地一致。"
- Quote: "内地居民都能办，即不受户籍地、居住地等条件限制，内地居民均可在异地申办出入境证件"
- Quote, same page: "今后内地居民不再需要为办证专程往返户籍地" / "有关公司企业外地员工出国出境不再需要回户籍地办理出入境证件"
- URL: https://ga.sz.gov.cn/JMHD/YWZSK/CRJGL_ZS/content/post_10660148.html (深圳市公安局, 发布日期 2023-06-20)
- Quote: "内地居民自2023年5月15日起，可以向全国任一公安机关出入境管理窗口申请办理中华人民共和国普通护照"
- The source is right that 2023-05-15 is when four kinds of 港澳 endorsements were added. Passports have been 全国通办 since 2019.
