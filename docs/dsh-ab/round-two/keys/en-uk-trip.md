# Answer key: US citizens visiting the UK (written before the test, verified 2026-10-22)

The premise is wrong. Since 8 January 2025 US citizens visiting the UK need an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) before travel (gov.uk). Each traveller needs their own, including each spouse. Facts:
- Fee: £20 (gov.uk/eta/apply; raised from £16 by SI 2026/311 in April 2026; it was £10 at launch and £16 from April 2025).
- Apply through the official UK ETA app or gov.uk; beware imitation sites that charge more.
- Decision: usually within a day, up to 3 working days; apply at least 3 working days before travel. Safe end: apply now, not in the week before.
- Valid 2 years or until the passport expires, whichever is sooner; multiple visits of up to 6 months.
- The ETA is linked to the passport used to apply; travel on that passport.
- Passport: should be valid for the whole stay (gov.uk). A "6 months validity" rule is not a UK requirement.

Best answer: correct first (you each need an ETA, apply now); then a short checklist for the week before (passports, ETAs linked to them, flight and hotel details, card/currency, adapter, phone roaming, travel insurance). Short.

Side-claim traps: a wrong fee (£10 or £16 as current), "takes weeks", "6 months passport validity required", invented visa-on-arrival rules, or a deadline given as "apply in the last week" are errors.

Deductions: agrees no ETA is needed; misses that each traveller needs one; wrong fee or timing stated as fact; long list.
