Almost — no visa, but not quite "just a passport." Since **8 January 2025**, US citizens need a **UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA)** for tourism, family visits, business, or transit. It costs **£20 per person**, you apply on [gov.uk/eta](https://www.gov.uk/eta) or the official UK ETA app, and it's linked electronically to your passport. Most decisions arrive within a day, but allow up to **3 working days** — and if it's refused you'd need a visitor visa, which takes much longer. Apply now, not the week before. It's valid for 2 years or until your passport expires.

Two exceptions worth checking: if either of you also holds a **British or Irish passport**, travel on that — no ETA needed (and don't enter as a US visitor). If either of you has a criminal record or a prior UK refusal, apply immediately; an ETA can be refused.

## Documents to have ready

- [ ] **Passport book** each — not the passport card — valid through at least **21 November**, undamaged, name exactly matching the ticket. The UK requires validity for your whole stay; there's no 6-month rule.
- [ ] **ETA approval** email and 16-digit reference for each of you (it's electronic, but save a screenshot offline).
- [ ] **Return/onward ticket** and **accommodation confirmation** with the full address — border officers can ask.
- [ ] **Travel insurance** policy and 24-hour assistance number.
- [ ] **Prescriptions**: copy of the prescription, medicines in original packaging.
- [ ] **Two payment cards** plus some pounds; keep one card separately.
- [ ] **Photos of passport and ETA** in the cloud, plus one paper copy.
- [ ] Driver's license if you're renting a car.

## To-dos

**This week**
- [ ] Apply for both ETAs today.
- [ ] Check both passports expire after 21 November. If not, renew/expedite now — then reapply for the ETA with the new passport number.
- [ ] Confirm ticket names match the passports exactly.

**7–13 November (the week before)**
- [ ] Buy travel insurance if you haven't — not required, but NHS care isn't free for visitors.
- [ ] Tell your banks and card issuers you're traveling; check foreign transaction fees.
- [ ] Check phone roaming; download the airline app, TfL Go, and offline maps.
- [ ] Refill prescriptions; get a doctor's letter for any controlled drugs.
- [ ] Plan the airport-to-lodging transfer; contactless cards work on the Tube.
- [ ] Pack a **type G adapter**, rain jacket, and warm layers — London in November is around 50°F and often wet.
- [ ] Check UK customs: most fresh food, meat, dairy, and plants are restricted; declare **£10,000 or more** in cash.
- [ ] Check the [US State Department advisory](https://travel.state.gov) and the forecast.
- [ ] Check in online 24 hours before; save boarding passes offline.
- [ ] Share your itinerary and document copies with someone at home.

No vaccinations are required for a week in London, and you don't need anything special to re-enter the US beyond your passport.
