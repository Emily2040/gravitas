Two things in that plan would land her in the ER, so I've corrected them before writing the text:

- **2 caplets (1,000 mg) every 4 hours = 6,000 mg a day.** The Extra Strength label caps adults at 6 caplets (3,000 mg) per 24 hours. At 78, with age-related lower liver and kidney clearance, 3,000 mg is the right ceiling, not 4,000.
- **NyQuil on top of that is a second acetaminophen dose** (325–650 mg depending on the form — check the label) plus doxylamine, a sedating antihistamine that geriatric guidelines specifically say to avoid over 65: falls, confusion, next-day grogginess. It doesn't shorten a cold either.

Here's the text, with the times:

```
Mom's pain plan for tonight — please follow exactly.

TYLENOL EXTRA STRENGTH 500 mg
• 8:00 pm — 2 caplets (1,000 mg)
• Overnight, only if she wakes in pain — 1 caplet (500 mg), never sooner than 4 hours after the last dose
• Tomorrow — 2 caplets at 8:00 am, 2:00 pm, 8:00 pm

HARD LIMIT: 6 caplets / 3,000 mg in any 24 hours. Count anything she has already taken today toward that total.

NO NYQUIL and nothing else containing acetaminophen — no cold/flu products, no combination pain pills, no leftover prescription painkillers — unless I confirm it first. Dentist said Tylenol only.

Cold: fluids, saline spray, humidifier, head propped up. Skip the sleep aids tonight.

Ice 20 min on, 20 min off for swelling. Soft food.

Call me if the pain isn't controlled, or call the dentist if there's new swelling, fever, or a bad taste/smell from the socket.
```

Keep the dentist's after-hours number where your brother can see it. If she has liver disease, kidney disease, or more than a drink or two a day, the ceiling is lower than 3,000 mg — worth a two-minute call to the dentist or pharmacist before tonight.
