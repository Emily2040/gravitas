**Almost: passport, yes — “just a passport,” no.** You don’t need a visa for a week of tourism (US citizens can visit for up to 6 months), but since January 2025 you do need a UK **Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA)** before you board. It isn’t a visa; it’s a pre-clearance tied to your passport, and the airline won’t let you fly without it.

**Do this now, not in departure week**
- Apply for each of you at [gov.uk/eta](https://www.gov.uk/eta) or the official UK ETA app: £20 each, passport scan and face photo, card payment. Use only the government site or app — lookalikes charge more.
- Decisions usually arrive within a day but can take 3 working days, and a refusal means reapplying with no refund. Don’t leave it to the last minute.
- It lasts 2 years (or until your passport expires) and covers multiple trips — [check](https://www.gov.uk/check-eta) whether you already have one from an earlier trip.
- Apply with the passport you’ll travel on. Renewing a passport later voids the ETA.

**Carry with you**
- Passport, valid through your return (~Nov 21). The UK rule is validity for your whole stay, not six months ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/uk-border-control/before-you-leave-for-the-uk)).
- ETA approval email with the 16-digit reference — screenshot it as backup; the ETA itself is electronic and linked to your passport.
- Return flight confirmation and your London address. Border officers may ask when you’re leaving and where you’re staying.
- Travel insurance policy and emergency number. Not required, but NHS hospital care can be charged to visitors.
- Copies of your passport photo pages and ETA emails, kept separately from the originals.
- Regular prescriptions in their original packaging, plus a copy of the prescription.

**The week before**
- Check both passports: expiry, no damage, name matches the ticket.
- Confirm both ETAs are approved and tied to the right passport number.
- Check in online as soon as it opens; save boarding passes offline.
- Buy travel insurance if you haven’t; note the policy number.
- Tell your bank and card issuer you’re traveling; check foreign-transaction fees. London is nearly cashless — contactless works on the Tube.
- Phone: international plan or eSIM; download the airline app, Citymapper, and offline maps.
- Book timed tickets you want (Tower of London, Churchill War Rooms, Sky Garden, theater).
- Pack a Type G adapter; expect 50s°F, rain, and sunset around 4 pm.
- Book your ride to Logan; be there 3 hours before an international flight.

**Worth flagging**
- If either passport expires soon, renew now. US processing takes weeks, and you’d need a fresh ETA afterward.
- If either of you has a criminal record or a prior UK refusal, the ETA may be refused — the route then is a Standard Visitor visa, which takes longer.
- Adding Paris or Amsterdam? That’s Schengen: separate 90/180-day rules, border biometrics (EES), and ETIAS once it launches.

The ETA is the only item with real lead time; everything else can wait for the week before.
