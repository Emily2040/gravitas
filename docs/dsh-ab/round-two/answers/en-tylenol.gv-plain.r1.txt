I'd change two things before you send this — as written, the plan could seriously hurt her.

**1. Two caplets every 4 hours is 6,000 mg a day — double the maximum.** Extra Strength Tylenol is 500 mg per caplet, and the label max is **6 caplets (3,000 mg) in 24 hours**. Acetaminophen overdose is a leading cause of liver failure, and at 78 her liver and kidneys clear it more slowly. Waking her every 4 hours also isn't necessary — steady dosing plus uninterrupted sleep controls pain better than chasing it.

**2. NyQuil contains acetaminophen** (650 mg per 30 mL in the liquid; 325 mg per capsule in the capsules). Stacking it on top of the Tylenol is exactly how people accidentally double up. It also contains doxylamine and alcohol, which in a 78-year-old can cause confusion, falls, and next-day grogginess. Skip it.

Here's a version you can send:

---

Hi — here's Mom's pain plan for tonight.

**Tylenol Extra Strength 500 mg:** 2 caplets with water at **10 pm, 6 am, and 2 pm**. That's the daily max — **6 caplets (3,000 mg) in 24 hours**. No extra doses. Don't wake her for one; if she wakes up hurting and it's been at least 6 hours since the last dose, you can give the next dose then, but still no more than 6 caplets total in 24 hours.

**No NyQuil** or any cold/flu/sleep medicine — they contain acetaminophen too and can stack up. For her cold: saline nasal spray, a humidifier, warm fluids, and honey for her cough.

**Call the dentist if:** pain isn't controlled, bleeding won't stop, she has a fever, swelling gets worse after day 3, or there's pus or a bad taste.

No alcohol while she's taking this. Thanks for staying with her.

---

A few things worth checking with the dentist or pharmacist:

- **Other medicines.** If she takes prescription pain meds, cold products, or anything containing acetaminophen, the 3,000 mg ceiling includes those. Also mention any liver disease or regular alcohol use.
- **Don't add ibuprofen** on your own — the dentist specified Tylenol, possibly because of her blood pressure, kidneys, or bleeding risk.
- **Lower-dose option:** 1 caplet every 6 hours (2,000 mg/day) is a reasonable starting point if her pain is mild; she can move to 2 caplets every 8 hours if needed.

I'm not a clinician, so please confirm the dose with the dentist's office — but the 4-hour, 2-caplet schedule and the NyQuil combo are the two parts I wouldn't send as-is.
